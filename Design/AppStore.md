## Description [4000]

Mihraab shows the day's prayer times for your island in the Maldives, quietly and beautifully.

Each prayer is painted in the colours of its sky: a hazy indigo dawn for Fajr, clear blue for Dhuhr, a burning sunset for Maghrib and a field of stars for Isha. A large countdown shows exactly how long until the next prayer, to the second.

BUILT FOR THE MALDIVES
• Every inhabited island across all 20 atolls
• Uses the local timetable zones and per-island minute offsets
• Always shows Maldives time, wherever your phone is

FIND YOUR ISLAND
• Search by island or atoll, or browse atoll by atoll
• Tap Use My Location to jump to the nearest island

REMINDERS THAT GET THROUGH
• Choose which prayers you want to be reminded of
• Reminders arrive as Time Sensitive notifications, so Focus modes don't hide them

HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
• Small widget: the next prayer with a live countdown
• Medium widget: the whole day's timetable, with the next prayer highlighted

ANY DAY OF THE YEAR
• Open the calendar to look up the timetable for any date

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• The full timetable is built into the app, so it works without internet
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking

Mihraab is free and open source.
