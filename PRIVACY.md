# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Mihraab is a prayer times app for the Maldives. It is built to work without collecting anything about you.

## What we collect

Nothing. Mihraab has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests, and no data leaves your device.

## Location

If you tap **Use My Location**, Mihraab asks iOS for your location once, finds the nearest island on your device, and then discards it. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere. You can use the app fully without granting location access by picking an island yourself.

## Notifications

Prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. No notification data is sent to any server.

## Data stored on your device

Mihraab saves your selected island and notification preferences on your device so the app and its widgets can show the right times. This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

## Children

Mihraab collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
